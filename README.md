# 📊 Sales & Profit Dashboard

## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales and profit performance using **Microsoft Excel**, transforming raw transactional data into an interactive dashboard. The dashboard tracks sales, profit, customer behavior, and regional performance across multiple years to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This project answers the following business questions:

- Which years and categories generate the most sales and profit?
- Which sub-categories drive the highest sales volume?
- Who are the top customers by revenue?
- How are sales distributed geographically across the US?
- How do sales trend month over month?
- What does the customer base look like year over year?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales & Total Profit KPIs
- Profit by Year (by Category)
- Sales by Sub-Category (Funnel Chart)
- Sales by State (Map Visualization)
- Customer by Year (Donut Chart)
- Top 5 Customers
- Sales by Month
- Interactive Filters (Year, Category, Month Slicers)

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- **Total Sales:** $1,928,888
- **Total Profit:** $247,961
- Identified top-performing sub-categories by sales volume (Phones, Chairs, Storage leading the pack).
- Pinpointed the top 5 highest-revenue customers.
- Mapped sales concentration across US states.
- Tracked customer volume trends across 2021–2024.
- Analyzed monthly sales seasonality.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing and retention efforts on top 5 customers driving disproportionate revenue.
- Prioritize inventory and promotions around top-performing sub-categories (Phones, Chairs, Storage).
- Investigate low-performing states for regional growth opportunities.
- Align seasonal staffing/inventory planning with monthly sales trends.
- Use category-level profit data to guide pricing and discounting strategy.
